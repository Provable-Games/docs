Files in this folder

page.txt and the track files are onchain output: each token's animation_url page
and the renderer's music segments, read from Starknet mainnet. cards/*.svg are
the tokens' metadata images. Official card and player outputs may be shared and
cached. See src/components/beastMusicManifest.json for the capture block and
hashes.

The Beast pixels and animations are CC0. The original music supplied through
Midi Fun retains its MIT terms.
