# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is Loot Survivor?**

Loot Survivor is a fully onchain dungeon crawler RPG built on Starknet. Every action, from combat to loot generation, happens directly on the blockchain, making the game transparent, verifiable, and permanent.

**Is Loot Survivor free to play?**

There are separate free testnet games for practice. Mainnet dungeons may require an entry fee or ticket, and mainnet transactions incur gas fees unless sponsorship is available. Free or sponsored mainnet entry still uses real contracts and is covered by the mainnet platform terms.

**What do I need to play?**

- **Free Testnet Version:** A supported browser; no personal wallet or real-value funds are required.
- **Mainnet Version:** A supported wallet or Game Account, any required entry fee or ticket, and funds for gas unless sponsorship is available. Wallet options and session permissions depend on the dungeon and client. Mainnet play is restricted to users aged 18 or older.

**Is the game mobile-friendly?**

Yes. Loot Survivor has a fully responsive design with a dedicated mobile interface optimized for touch controls.

## Gameplay

**How do I start playing?**

Choose your game mode and click "New Game" to start exploring. See the [Getting Started Guide](/lootsurvivor/guide/getting-started) for detailed instructions.

**What happens when my adventurer dies?**

Death is permanent — your adventurer's journey ends but earned assets remain onchain forever. Start a new adventure anytime.

**Can I have multiple adventurers?**

Yes. Each adventurer is a separate onchain token with unique progression.

**How does combat work?**

Turn-based combat with rock-paper-scissors type advantages (plus or minus 50% damage). Each weapon/armor type beats one and loses to another. See: [Battle Guide](/lootsurvivor/guide/battle) and [Combat Mechanics](/lootsurvivor/combat).

**What are the different stats?**

Seven core stats: STR (damage), DEX (flee), VIT (HP), INT (obstacles), WIS (ambush), CHA (prices), LUCK (crits). Each affects combat and exploration differently. Full details: [Stats Guide](/lootsurvivor/stats).

**How do items work?**

5 tiers (T5 common to T1 legendary). Items level up and unlock suffixes (Lv15) and prefixes (Lv19+) for bonuses. See: [Loot Guide](/lootsurvivor/loot).

**What's the best strategy?**

No single best strategy. Focus on type advantages, stat balance, tactical decisions, and resource management. See: [Battle Guide](/lootsurvivor/guide/battle).

## Technical

**What blockchain is Loot Survivor on?**

Loot Survivor runs on Starknet, a Layer 2 network for Ethereum. Fees and confirmation times vary. Network security does not guarantee the safety of the game's contracts, accounts, sessions or client software; see the unaudited-software disclosure below.

**Are the game outcomes truly random?**

The randomness mechanism depends on the dungeon. Some use a VRF (Verifiable Random Function); others use a commit-reveal system based on future block hashes. Review the rules and deployed contracts for the dungeon you choose.

**Can I verify the game logic?**

You can inspect published source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/Provable-Games/death-mountain) and deployed contracts and transactions through Starknet explorers. Check that the source and addresses correspond to the dungeon you use, including any upgrades. Source publication or explorer verification is not a security audit.

**How are gas fees kept low?**

Optimizations include packed storage, batch operations, and efficient algorithms, plus Starknet's L2 scaling benefits.

## Economic

**How does the market work?**

The in-game market refreshes when you level up, uses VRF seeds for item generation, and prices decrease with Charisma. No real money transactions between players.

**Can I trade items with other players?**

Currently, direct player-to-player trading is not implemented. All trading happens through the in-game market system.

**Are there rewards for playing?**

Yes. When you kill beasts during gameplay, you collect them and earn $SURVIVOR tokens on collection. [Budokan](/budokan) tournaments with prize pools are another way to earn rewards. Check [Discord](https://discord.gg/Q36rUxS66c) for current tournament info and schedules.

## Wallet & Account

**Which wallets are supported?**

Use the wallet options offered by the current client for your chosen dungeon. These can include Cartridge Controller and, where available, the experimental session-enabled Game Account. Other external-wallet features depend on the operation. Review account ownership, session permissions and any spending limits before authorizing access.

**I lost access to my wallet. Can I recover my adventurer?**

If you lose wallet access, you lose access to your adventurers. Always keep your recovery phrase secure and backed up.

**Can I play on multiple devices?**

Yes. Your adventurers are tied to your wallet address, not your device. Play from any device where you can access your wallet.

**Why do transactions sometimes fail?**

Common reasons include network congestion and wallet not being properly connected.

## Advanced

**Can I build on top of Loot Survivor?**

Yes. Loot Survivor is open source and designed to be composable. You can build custom frontends, create analytical tools, extend game mechanics, and integrate with other protocols.

**How do I integrate Loot Survivor into my app?**

See our [Integration Guide](/lootsurvivor/technical/integration) for detailed instructions on building with Loot Survivor's contracts.

**What's the maximum level?**

There is no hard level cap. The theoretical maximum depends on your ability to survive increasingly difficult challenges.

**How are beasts generated?**

Beasts are generated from VRF seeds based on your adventurer's level, a random seed value, and tier distribution tables. See: [Combat Mechanics](/lootsurvivor/combat).

**Can I predict market items?**

No. Market items use VRF seeds for true randomness (unless configured otherwise in settings).

## Troubleshooting

**The game won't load**

Try these steps: clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Del), disable ad blockers (most common cause of wallet connection issues), try Chrome or Firefox, check [status.starknet.io](https://status.starknet.io), and ensure JavaScript is enabled.

**Transactions are stuck**

Wait for network congestion to clear, check block explorer, refresh page, or reconnect wallet. More help: [Getting Started Guide](/lootsurvivor/guide/getting-started).

**I can't connect my wallet**

Ensure correct controller login details, check network, refresh page, or try a different browser. Setup help: [Getting Started Guide](/lootsurvivor/guide/getting-started).

**The game shows incorrect data**

This is a sync issue. Refresh the page, clear cache, reconnect wallet, or wait for the indexer.

## Legal & Safety

**Is Loot Survivor audited?**

The platform's contracts, including the session-enabled Game Account, have not undergone a formal security audit. The software is experimental. Published or verified source code is not a security audit or a guarantee of safety. Bugs, vulnerabilities and signing mistakes can cause the permanent loss of all funds you expose. Review the contracts and transactions you authorize, including session permissions. Provable Games, Inc. cannot reverse blockchain transactions or guarantee recovery and does not offer compensation for losses, subject to rights that applicable law does not allow you to waive. Read the [Mainnet Platform Terms of Service](https://www.lootsurvivor.io/terms), including the public version archive, before connecting a wallet.

**How does gameplay work?**

Loot Survivor combines player decisions with random elements. Your choices about equipment, combat and exploration affect each run. Dungeon and tournament rules explain the available actions, scoring and rewards.

**What data is collected?**

Wallet addresses and transactions are publicly recorded onchain. Technical data and, where permitted or consented to, analytics data may also be processed off-chain. When you use the signed agreement process, Provable Games, Inc. stores the message and signature, wallet and account-authority information, the exact legal-document versions, verification results and timestamps privately off-chain. The [mainnet privacy policy](https://provable.games/privacy/paid) explains the purposes, retention and your rights. Promotions or identity verification can involve additional personal data under separate policies, including the [KYC privacy policy](https://provable.games/privacy/kyc).

**Why am I asked to sign the terms?**

Where enabled, the platform asks your wallet owner to sign a message recording agreement to a specific terms version and receipt of the privacy notice. This message authorizes no transaction, transfer, token approval or gameplay session. The platform confirms agreement after the service verifies and stores the record; a browser checkbox alone is not a server signature record. You can read the documents before signing and download your own evidence privately. Optional analytics choices stay separate. Declining leaves public information and limited existing-asset recovery available; new platform activity requires agreement.

**Can I lose money playing?**

Yes. Some modes charge entry fees, rewards are not guaranteed, and software or contract failures can put more than an entry fee at risk, including assets exposed through your account or approvals. Never deposit, approve or spend more than you can afford to lose. See the [platform terms](https://www.lootsurvivor.io/terms) for the full risk disclosures and liability provisions. Mainnet platform participation is restricted to users aged 18 or older, including free mainnet entries; separate free testnet games have their own terms.
