### How It Works

1. **Find the Beast**: "Pain Whisper" Warlock spawns randomly in the dungeon
2. **Defeat and collect it**: Defeating it lets you collect the [Beast NFT](/lootsurvivor/beasts/collectibles)
3. **Claim the STRK**: The owner of the Pain Whisper NFT claims the 33,333 STRK onchain from the Beast Mode contract
4. **One payout**: The bounty can be claimed once for that Beast

### Promotion Period

This promotion runs until Pain Whisper's bounty has been claimed.
