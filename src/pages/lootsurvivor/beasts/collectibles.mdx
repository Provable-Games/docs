# Musical Onchain Beasts

Beast collectibles are **Musical Onchain Beasts** (symbol **MOB**) on Starknet: fully onchain cards with animated art, card grades and [music](/lootsurvivor/beasts/music). There are more than 86,000 Beasts across 75 species, and those species can hold at most 93,225 live Beasts, 1,243 per species including its Genesis.

<BeastRarityCards />

## Earning a Beast

Be the first to defeat a named Beast in [Beast Mode](/lootsurvivor/guide/getting-started).

- **Preview:** when a collectable Beast appears, the game client shows the grade it would have.
- **Claim:** after the kill, the client sends the claim for you as a separate onchain transaction. The NFT goes to the adventurer's owner.
- **One at a time:** only one live token can exist per species, prefix and suffix, so a Beast that's already collected can't be claimed again. Burning a Beast frees that name, except Genesis, which can't be burned.

See the [prefix reference](/lootsurvivor/loot/prefixes) for every prefix and suffix.

## [Survivor Token](/lootsurvivor/token) Reward

The first collection of a species, prefix and suffix pays SURVIVOR by tier:

| Tier | SURVIVOR |
| --- | --- |
| T1 | 14 |
| T2 | 12 |

A later remint of the same Beast pays nothing. A kill of a remapped species pays nothing.

## Rarity

These names are the labels used in these docs. Metadata only shows `Visual Effects: 0–3`.

| Name | Preset | Odds | Art and rim | Music page | Sound effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Regular** | 0 | 89.75% | Regular GIF, gold rim, tier logo | Standard | Written |
| **Shiny** | 1 | 5% | Shiny GIF, rainbow rim and logo | Standard | Mega |
| **MIDI** | 2 | 5% | Regular GIF, gold rim, tier logo | Notes background | Echo |
| **Shiny + MIDI** | 3 | 0.25% | Shiny GIF, rainbow rim and logo | Notes background | Mega + echo |

Every Original is animated. MIDI means the piano-roll notes drawn behind the art in the token's [music player](/lootsurvivor/beasts/music), as in the examples above. Marketplace images never show them, so a MIDI card looks the same as a Regular card there.

## Card Grade

Every Beast has a condition, like a printed trading card, and a **Card Grade** from 1 to 10 derived from it. The defects are visible on the card: centering, hickeys, voids, roller marks, and edge and corner chips. Grade 10 means no defects with centering of 55/45 or better.

### How to read the traits

| Trait | Values | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Card Grade` | 1–10 | The overall grade |
| `Horizontal Centering`, `Vertical Centering` | e.g. `56.725/43.275` | Border ratio, larger side first |
| `Horizontal Shift`, `Vertical Shift` | `Centered`, `Left`/`Right`, `Up`/`Down` | Which way the print moved |
| `Hickeys` | 0–3 | Number of hickeys |
| `Voids` | `None`, `Low`, `Medium`, `High` | Severity of ink voids |
| `Roller Marks`, `Edge Chips`, `Corner Chips` | `None`, `Light`, `Moderate`, `Heavy` | Severity of each defect |
| `Hickey Location`, `Void Location`, `Roller Location` | `None`, `Outside Art`, `In Art` | Where the defect sits |

## Rankings and the King

Each species is ranked by power, then health, then earliest mint block. Rankings are updated separately from minting, and anyone can trigger an update. A new Beast shows Rank 0 until the next update. Genesis is always Rank 0.

The **King** is the species' current Rank 1 Beast. Its owner controls the [Species Song's sound mode and effects](/lootsurvivor/beasts/music#who-controls-what).

## Metadata and Provenance

Art and metadata are generated by the contracts. Alongside the grade and rarity traits, each token reports current onchain state:

| Trait | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Beast`, `Prefix`, `Suffix`, `Type`, `Tier` | Identity |
| `Level`, `Health`, `Power`, `Rank` | Combat stats and current rank |
| `Adventurers Killed` | Adventurers this Beast has killed |
| `Last Killed By` | The adventurer who last killed this Beast |
| `Minted`, `Mint Block` | When it was minted, as a timestamp and block |
| `Genesis` | 1 for a Genesis Beast, otherwise 0 |

## Autographs

A holder can ask a registered signer for an autograph, and the signer approves it onchain. A completed autograph appears on the card and stays with it through transfers.

## Ownership

Beasts are transferable ERC-721 tokens. Original species carry a 5% royalty to the Survivor DAO.
