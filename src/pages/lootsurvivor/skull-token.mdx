## Overview

$SKULL is an ERC20 token on Starknet that represents beast kills in Loot Survivor. Each verified kill credited to your Beast lets you claim 1 $SKULL. You can spend it on [music tracks](/lootsurvivor/beasts/music#buying-tracks) for any Beast.

### At a Glance

- **Standard**: ERC20 on Starknet
- **Supply**: Uncapped — minted continuously as adventurers are killed in Loot Survivor
- **Earning**: 1 $SKULL per verified kill credited to your Beast
- **Uses**: [Beast music tracks](/lootsurvivor/beasts/music#buying-tracks) (each burns exactly 1 $SKULL)
- **Contract Address**: `0x2798a8dc91bf1ef502cf8710751d26128fdf8e9d0823522fc08632ecc9172e8`
- **Legacy V1 Address**: `0x01c3c8284d7eed443b42f47e764032a56eaf50a9079d67993b633930e3689814`

## How to Earn $SKULL

$SKULL tokens are earned exclusively through [Loot Survivor](/lootsurvivor) gameplay:

- Own a Beast of an eligible species (Genesis Beasts can't claim)
- When your Beast kills an adventurer and the kill is credited through its dungeon's verifier, you can claim **1 $SKULL**
- Unclaimed $SKULL stays with the Beast's identity, so it moves with the NFT

The more beasts you own and the stronger they are (tier 1 beasts kill most adventurers), the more adventurers they'll defeat and the more $SKULL you'll accumulate.

### What Counts

$SKULL V2 pays only for verified kills credited to the Beast on the Beasts NFT. Death Mountain history isn't counted, because SKULL V1 already paid for it. A newly minted identity starts with zero credited kills, though an eligible earlier run can be credited after minting if a trusted verifier confirms it. Burning and reminting an identity keeps its credited kills and what it has already claimed.

V1 $SKULL can be converted 1:1 into V2 through the contract's `convert` function.

## What $SKULL Is Used For

$SKULL buys music tracks. Anyone can buy a track for any Beast, and each purchase burns exactly 1 $SKULL. See [Buying tracks](/lootsurvivor/beasts/music#buying-tracks).

## Token Economics

$SKULL connects Loot Survivor gameplay to Beast music:

1. **Collect beasts** → Own Beasts through [Loot Survivor gameplay](/lootsurvivor/beasts/collectibles) or a secondary market
2. **Beasts kill adventurers** → Earn $SKULL from verified kills in Loot Survivor
3. **Spend $SKULL** → Buy music tracks for any Beast

Since $SKULL has no supply cap, the token continues to be minted as long as Loot Survivor is played. More players means more kills and more $SKULL, while every track purchase burns 1 $SKULL.
